Real Talk From Someone Actually in the Program

I’m currently a student at Tulsa Welding School. I just finished the hands-on Professional Welders Course and I’m now working through the AOS Welder Inspections track. Before that, I spent time in the Marine Corps with a couple combat deployments.

This list isn’t theory — it’s the gear I actually use and recommend to other students who want equipment that performs well in a demanding school environment and will still be solid when they hit the workforce. I focused on reliability, protection, and things that make daily shop life easier.

Essential Personal Protective Equipment

Auto-Darkening Welding Helmet

A good auto-darkening helmet is the single most important piece of gear you’ll buy. Look for true color technology, a wide viewing area, and reliable sensors. This protects your eyes and face from UV/IR radiation on every process you’ll run at TWS (SMAW, GMAW, GTAW).

My Recommended Beginner Welding Gear Checklist

  • Auto-darkening welding helmet (true color preferred)
  • Heavy-duty welding gloves (MIG/stick)
  • Welding jacket or leather sleeves + apron
  • Safety glasses with side shields
  • Welding cap / skull cap
  • Chipping hammer + stainless wire brush
  • Soapstone + holder
  • Strong welding magnets + clamps
  • MIG pliers / Welpers
  • 4½" or 5" angle grinder + discs
  • Half-mask respirator + P100 filters
  • Ear protection
  • Layout tools (combination square, speed square, tape, center punch)
  • Tool bag or toolbox

Final Word

Start with the helmet, gloves, and FR clothing — those are non-negotiable for safety and daily shop work. Then add the tools that make your life easier (chipping hammer, magnets, MIG pliers, grinder).

Buy once with quality in mind where it counts (helmet, gloves, respirator). The gear on this list will get you through welding school and still be useful when you’re working in the field.

Stay consistent, stay safe, and take care of your body. The trades will reward you if you show up prepared and respect the craft.
