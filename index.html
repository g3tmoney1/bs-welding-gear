B’s Welding Student Starter Kit

Starting welding school can feel confusing because everybody has an opinion on what gear you need. Some stuff is necessary. Some stuff can wait. Some stuff is just a waste of money when you are first starting out.

I made this list for beginners like me who are learning, practicing, and trying to get better without buying every tool in the world on day one.

This is not about looking fancy. This is about showing up prepared, staying safe, and having the basic gear that actually helps in class or in the garage.

1. Auto-Darkening Welding Helmet

A welding helmet is one of the first things I would get. An auto-darkening hood makes it easier to see where you are starting, especially when you are still learning how to strike an arc and hold steady.

For a beginner, look for adjustable shade, grind mode, comfortable headgear, and clear lens.

Good for: welding school, stick, MIG, flux-core, garage work.

Watch out for: cheap headgear or poor visibility.
